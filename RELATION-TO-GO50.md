# RELATION TO GO-50

Two ontologies, two rings, no duplication.

- **GO-50** (50 terms): the *operator cognition* ontology. What the operator
  sees and operates on: ENTITY, CLAIM, EVIDENCE, LOVE, TERNARY_STATE, etc.
  Validated by the 5×10 fidelity exam. Ring: Global(50) → Domain(≤25) →
  Client(≤25).
- **System Ontology** (15 primitives): the *machine* ontology. What the
  software is made of: INFORMATION, IDENTITY, STATE, EVENT, TIME,
  RELATIONSHIP, CAPABILITY, AUTHORITY, EVIDENCE, POLICY, EXECUTION,
  RESOURCE, BOUNDARY, PROTOCOL, ARTIFACT.

The bridge: GO-50's EVIDENCE/CLAIM/VERDICT terms describe the operator's
epistemics; the System Ontology's EVIDENCE/POLICY/AUTHORITY primitives describe
the machine that must honor them. When the verifier mints a receipt, GO-50
scores the verdict and the System Ontology describes the mechanism. Neither
replaces the other.

Rule: a term that appears in both keeps one definition per ring and a
recorded mapping — never two competing definitions.
